Two rounds of test users plus independent code audits, 25 November 2026. Every serious and medium problem found is fixed. Three decisions are yours (below).

Where the problems were

Decisions for you

  1. The "(re: …)" aside. When you write only "lol that" or "this", NoteSmith can quote what was being said just before, shown apart from your words. The quote is always really said, which code checks, but which line you reacted to is a guess, and testers caught wrong guesses. I recommend: keep it off by default (as now). Remove it if you never turn it on.
  2. Corrections and doubt. If you write "fired at 24 right? paid 20B i think" and later "actually 21, and 2B", the note becomes "fired at 21. Facebook paid 2 billion for it. (Corrected from 24 and 20 billion.)" The "right?" and "I think" go, because your correction settled them. The judging AI disagrees: it wants the doubt kept. I recommend: keep it as is.
  3. Misheard names. "andrew" (the captions' spelling) now stays as you wrote it, sometimes with the AI's guess marked: "Andrew (Anduril?)". It no longer swaps your word silently. I recommend: keep.
  4. Try it yourself. The test guide is human_feedback/round-2026-11-25-v2/index.html. Ten minutes on a video you actually want to watch matters most.

Four new test users and code auditors, after round 1's fixes. Serious and medium findings below. All are in how you use it now unless marked. All fixed and checked again.

serious Undo could erase newer work

If you deleted a scribble, added another within 10 seconds, then pressed Undo, the new one vanished. It happened rarely, but it lost work silently. It had been there from the start, and the new Ctrl+Z shortcut made it easier to hit. Fixed, and checked by script.

serious A swapped name and an added fact

The AI replaced a name you wrote with the one it thought you meant, and added "sold", which you never wrote. These are small, but they're exactly what NoteSmith promises not to do. It happened in a few notes per session. Fixed and covered by the quality test.

serious Corrections lost or invented details

Merging "actually it was 21" into the earlier note dropped "got fired", and once invented what you'd "first written". Fixed. Corrections now keep everything else, and the bracket names only what you really wrote.
